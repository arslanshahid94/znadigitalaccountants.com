Frequently asked questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask before signing on.

Which UK services do you cover?

Self Assessment, rental accounts, company year-end and dormant accounts, management accounts, Corporation Tax, VAT, PAYE, HMRC and Companies House filing, and company secretarial services. We also provide bookkeeping, bank reconciliation and QuickBooks set-up for UK and US clients.

Can you handle clients in both the US and UK?

Yes, this is a core part of what we do. We handle US bookkeeping, payroll and reconciliation alongside UK VAT, PAYE, Corporation Tax, Self Assessment, HMRC and Companies House requirements, often for the same multi-entity business.

What do I need to send you to get started?

Usually your bank statements or a bank feed connection, sales and purchase invoices or receipts, and last year's accounts or returns if you have them. If anything is missing, we tell you exactly what to send.

How quickly can we get started?

Most clients are onboarded within 2–3 business days of sharing access to their bank feeds or accounting software. There is no long setup process.

Do you work with QuickBooks Online specifically?

Yes, QuickBooks Online is our default working file. We can set it up from scratch, clean up an existing file, or migrate you from spreadsheets or another platform.

How do you keep our financial data secure?

We work through your existing accounting software and bank-feed connections rather than storing sensitive data ourselves, and access is limited to what's needed for your engagement.

What does a free consultation involve?

A short call or email exchange where we review your current setup and tell you plainly what's needed to get it current and compliant, with no obligation to sign on afterwards.

Send over your current setup and we'll tell you plainly what's needed to get it current and compliant. No obligation, and we reply within one business day.
