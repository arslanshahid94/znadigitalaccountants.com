Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask about this service.

Can you fix my existing QuickBooks file?

Yes. We review it first and tell you what needs correcting and what is best left alone. Then we clean it up and document the changes.

Is QuickBooks suitable for both UK and US businesses?

QuickBooks Online has region-specific versions, so a UK company and a US company use separate files with the right tax settings. We set up each file correctly.

Do you run the books after set-up?

If you would like us to. Many clients move from set-up to ongoing bookkeeping and reconciliation, but set-up is available on its own.
