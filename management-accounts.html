Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask about this service.

What is the difference between management accounts and statutory accounts?

Statutory accounts are the annual set filed with Companies House and used for tax. They follow set rules and look backwards. Management accounts are for you: more frequent, more detailed and shaped around how you run the business.

I already have bookkeeping. Do I need management accounts?

Bookkeeping records the transactions. Management accounts summarise them into figures you can decide from. If you are only reading a bank balance or a list of invoices, a monthly summary is usually the next step.

Can the reports come from QuickBooks?

Yes. QuickBooks Online is our default working file, and reports can be run from it or supplied as a separate summary.
