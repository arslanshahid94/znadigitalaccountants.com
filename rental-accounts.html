Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask about this service.

Can I claim for repairs and improvements?

Repairs that keep a property in its current condition are generally deductible against rental income. Improvements that add to the property are usually capital costs and are treated differently. We review each larger item and tell you how it falls.

What if my properties are held in a limited company?

Then the company files its own accounts and Corporation Tax return instead of you reporting that income on Self Assessment. We cover both routes, so tell us how the property is held and we will set it up the right way.

How often should rental records be updated?

Monthly or quarterly works for most landlords. Regular updates mean the year-end return is a matter of checking figures, not rebuilding them.
