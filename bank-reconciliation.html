Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask about this service.

Why does daily reconciliation matter?

Errors are easiest to fix when they are a day old. Duplicate payments, missing receipts and fraud show up sooner, and your reports stay close to real time.

What if the books do not match the bank?

We trace the difference to its source and correct what is wrong. Anything we cannot resolve goes on a short list, so you know exactly which document or explanation we need.

Can you reconcile earlier months?

Yes. We can work through previous months or years and report what we found.
