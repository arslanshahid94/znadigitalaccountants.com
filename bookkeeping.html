Common questions

Straight answers to what clients usually ask about this service.

Which software do you use?

QuickBooks Online is our default working file. If you already use something else, tell us and we will say plainly whether we can work in it or would suggest a move.

How long does onboarding take?

Most clients are onboarded within 2 to 3 business days of sharing access to their bank feeds or accounting software. There is no long setup process.

How do you keep our financial data secure?

We work through your existing accounting software and bank-feed connections rather than storing sensitive data ourselves, and access is limited to what is needed for your engagement.
